What changed

v2.1 Nov 10, 2026

New track: Sales Eng. A buddy of mine just got hit with layoffs (infosec sales engineer, battle tested as a consultant, superb guy), and when I went looking for SE gigs on the Splat there weren't any. "Sales Engineer" didn't match a single rule, so those jobs never made it onto the page.

Now there's a Sales Eng button: sales engineers, solutions engineers and consultants, pre-sales, and the solutions architects at vendors like AWS and NVIDIA. Pick it and the Kind row splits them by what you'd be selling: Security, Cloud & infra, or Software & data. For infosec, that's Sales Eng plus Security. A test run tonight found 210 of them, 30 in security.

A few caveats. "Sales Engineer" is just as often valves or HVAC as software, so every one of these gets checked by the classifier before it shows up, and if the classifier is down some morning they wait a day. About 30 solutions engineers and architects that were under Cyber or Infra & IT moved over here. And every listing gets checked once more with the new question, so a few may switch tracks or drop off (mostly plain software jobs that shouldn't have been here anyway).

Other stuff

  • The "Tell me what would help" form has a Sales Eng box too.
  • On phones the track buttons put their counts under the names, so all five still fit.

v2.0 Nov 9, 2026

Dystopia Check? Check!

Every card has a "Company check" button now. Click it and you get the public record on whoever's hiring: their federal contracts (and how much of that came from the Pentagon, DHS, ICE and CBP), charges filed against them at the labor board, employment discrimination cases, what their PAC gave to each party, and a link to look them up on Violation Tracker.

A few honest caveats. The biggest 180 or so employers are checked against their real legal names and federal contractor IDs, and more get added every week. Everyone else gets matched on the name on the card, and the check tells you that. A charge or a lawsuit is an allegation, not a verdict. And the court cases are only for the reviewed employers, and they take a week or so to fill in, because the free court search only lets me look up so many a day.

If something looks wrong, tell me in Discord and I'll fix it.

Other stuff from today

  • Cards actually read the posting now (most of them, so far): pay, clearance, days in the office, whether remote is limited to some states, the degree and years they want, and whether you'd be managing people. Pick a track and you can narrow it by the kind of work, too.
  • Search doesn't eat your spaces anymore (thanks to whoever reported that one), and "help desk" finds "service desk" too.
  • If you send the form without an email, I can't write back, so now it tells you where to find me.
  • Fixed the dead Discord links.
  • Last night: "New" counts from your last visit if you've been gone a while, there's a Last 7 days filter, and the filter buttons tell you what they do.

Earlier versions weren't written up. Every change is public in the repo's history.
