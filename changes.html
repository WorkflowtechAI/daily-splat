What changed

v2.0 Nov 9, 2026

Big one today: every job card has a "Company check" button now. Click it and you get the public record on that employer:

  • Federal contracts for the last three fiscal years, and how much of it the Defense Department, DHS, ICE and CBP paid for
  • Unfair-labor-practice charges at the NLRB, and how they closed
  • Federal employment discrimination cases (this part fills in over the next few days)
  • What their corporate PAC gave candidates, by party (that's employee money; a company can't give to federal candidates directly)
  • A link to their page on Violation Tracker

So yes, you can check whether it's Palantir before you apply.

About 180 of the busiest employers are reviewed by legal name and federal contractor ID, and more get added every week. Everyone else is matched automatically on the name on the card, and the panel tells you that and shows what it matched. A charge or a lawsuit is an allegation, not a finding. If you spot a wrong match, tell me on Discord and I'll fix it.

Also today

  • Cards show what the posting actually says: pay, clearance, days in the office, where a remote hire can live, the degree and years they ask for, and whether you'd manage people. Pick a track and a "Kind" row lets you narrow it down (SecOps, AppSec, Help desk, Project & program vs Product, and so on).
  • Search keeps your spaces now, and "help desk" finds "service desk" too. Thanks to whoever reported that one.
  • If you send the "Tell me what would help" form without an email, it now tells you how to reach me, since I can't write back otherwise.
  • The Discord link was dead in a couple of places. Fixed everywhere: discord.gg/D9pHEghG9C
  • From last night: if you've been away, "New" counts from your last visit; there's a "Last 7 days" filter; and the filter buttons explain what they do.

Questions, ideas, or something looks off? Drop it in Discord.

Earlier versions weren't written up. Every change is public in the repo's history.
