What changed

v3.0 Nov 12, 2026

New in the Company check: "Open roles on its own board." Somebody in the chat asked whether Vultr's 60-odd open roles were real jobs or placeholders, and the Company check had nothing to say about hiring. Now it does. For an employer whose cards link to its own Ashby, Greenhouse or Lever board, it shows how many listings are on that board (all teams and locations, not just what's on the Splat) and how long they've been up, by the board's own dates. Vultr's board had 62 on Nov 12, and about half went up in the last 30 days.

It also keeps a daily record of each board now, so it can count roles that get taken down and listed again. That needs history, so those numbers show up once it has 90 days of it, around February. None of this says a job is fake. A role can stay up a long time because it's hard to fill or because one listing covers a bunch of hires, and the panel says so. Workday and companies' own careers sites aren't covered yet.

Fixed some dates, too. A feed can show a newer date than the employer's own board does: Vultr's Technical Support Specialist has been up since March and showed here as posted Nov 6. When the Splat has the employer's board dates, a card now goes by the earlier one, and anything older than 30 days (14 for remote) comes off like it should. Vultr's own board is read directly now too.

Other stuff

  • Vultr's Violation Tracker link was searching the name of an unrelated Georgia company that shares its legal name. It searches Vultr's former name, Choopa, now.

v2.4 Nov 11, 2026

New: Share a job. Found something good that isn't on the Splat? There's a "Share a job" button up top now. Paste the link and send it, that's it. The Splat reads the posting for the title, the employer and where it is, and tells you right away if it's outside the tracks or the area. For a LinkedIn or Indeed link it asks you for those, since it doesn't read those sites. Otherwise it shows up after the next morning's run.

LinkedIn and Indeed links work too. The Splat never copies those sites, so it looks for the employer's own posting first and links to that. If there isn't one, the card says "LinkedIn only" so you know what you're clicking into, and it comes off after two weeks. A link to a company's own careers site waits until I've looked at it, so nobody can slip a fake job page onto the Splat. Shared jobs go through the same checks as everything else, and nothing about you gets kept.

v2.3 Nov 11, 2026

Federal jobs are on the Splat now, straight from USAJOBS: IT jobs open to the public in Oregon, Washington and Idaho, like Bonneville Power, FEMA in Bothell, the VA and the Navy over in Bangor. It's only a handful at a time (9 in a test today), and every one links to its real posting on usajobs.gov. None of the federal IT jobs posted this month are remote, so don't go looking for them under Remote.

v2.2 Nov 11, 2026

Remote means remote now. If a listing says remote but the employer's own posting says hybrid or in the office, it's off the Splat. That was 53 jobs today, mostly from one feed's remote label. Same bait-and-switch somebody posted about on LinkedIn this week: a "remote" job behind two email walls that turned out to be in an office. If a real remote job disappears, tell me.

The Splat already links straight to the employer for 97% of jobs. The rest go to Jobicy, Himalayas or RemoteOK, and the card says so. Anything that would send you through Dice, LinkedIn, Indeed or the like before you reach the employer stays off.

City of Portland, TriMet, Port of Portland, Beaverton and Metro jobs are off the Splat. Their job site, governmentjobs.com, changed its terms to say nobody gets to copy those listings, and that's their call. It was only a handful at a time anyway. The links to their job pages are under "How this works," so check them directly.

v2.1 Nov 10, 2026

New track: Sales Eng. By request.

Now there’s a Sales Eng button: sales engineers, solutions engineers and consultants, pre-sales, and the solutions architects at vendors like AWS and NVIDIA. Pick it and the Kind row splits them by what you’d be selling: Security, Cloud & infra, or Software & data. For infosec, that’s Sales Eng plus Security. A test run tonight found about 175 of them, 31 in security.

A few caveats. “Sales Engineer” is just as often valves or HVAC as software, and plenty of “Solutions Architect” jobs are really support or install work after the sale, so every one of these gets checked twice by the classifier: once on the title, then again on the posting itself. If the classifier is down some morning, new ones wait a day. The test run got checked listing by listing and it’s about 93% right, so a few will still slip through; tell me when you see one. Some solutions engineers and architects that were under Cyber or Infra & IT moved over here. And every listing gets checked once more with the new question, so a few may switch tracks or drop off (mostly plain software jobs that shouldn’t have been here anyway).

v2.0 Nov 9, 2026

Dystopia Check? Check!

Every card has a "Company check" button now. Click it and you get the public record on whoever's hiring: their federal contracts (and how much of that came from the Pentagon, DHS, ICE and CBP), charges filed against them at the labor board, employment discrimination cases, what their PAC gave to each party, and a link to look them up on Violation Tracker.

A few honest caveats. The biggest 180 or so employers are checked against their real legal names and federal contractor IDs, and more get added every week. Everyone else gets matched on the name on the card, and the check tells you that. A charge or a lawsuit is an allegation, not a verdict. And the court cases are only for the reviewed employers, and they take a week or so to fill in, because the free court search only lets me look up so many a day.

If something looks wrong, tell me in Discord and I'll fix it.
