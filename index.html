How this works

Every morning a script on this server reads company job boards directly (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday boards for PNW employers and security vendors, plus Amazon) and the open aggregators listed in the job source directory: Foorilla, Remotive, RemoteOK, Jobicy, Himalayas, Working Nomads and The Muse.

It keeps a posting when the title reads as security, or as cloud, network, systems, sysadmin, help desk or support engineering (general software development is left out), the level is anything from entry to principal (no internships, no directors), and the location is in WA, OR or ID or remote within the US. Each new posting is then checked by a small classifier (Jev) for track and level, which catches titles the rules miss and drops ones that only look right. Recruiter and staffing-agency reposts are left out; the page lists employers.

A job drops off when its source stops listing it, when it is older than 30 days (14 for remote), or when Foorilla marks it expired. If a whole source fails one morning, its jobs are kept for up to two days rather than vanishing. Applying is always on you: every link goes to the employer's posting or the aggregator's free listing.
